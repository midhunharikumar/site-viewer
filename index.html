About flatmap.cloud (BengaluruValue)

flatmap.cloud is an independent, free Bangalore (Bengaluru) real-estate intelligence site — it maps residential property price trends, 3-year projections, Namma Metro connectivity and neighbourhood livability across 127 Bengaluru localities and 168 tracked tier-1 new-launch projects. Snapshot as of June 2026.

Note on the domain name: flatmap.cloud was previously used by an unrelated data-annotation/ML preprocessing service. The domain was repurposed in 2026 for this Bengaluru property project. If you're looking for the old service, it has shut down — this is a different project at the same domain.

Data is modeled, not official

Figures are best-effort real + modeled estimates. Current prices and appreciation are anchored to public sources (99acres, Magicbricks, Anarock, Knight Frank, PropTiger, NHB); intermediate quarters are interpolated, and 2026–2029 figures are projected scenarios, not forecasts. Livability (schools, water stress, flood, safety) and Cauvery-supply status are relative research-based ratings, not official statistics. The city-wide heatmap is a spatial interpolation between locality centers. Metro, project and "vision" coordinates are approximate; the dotted "Vision" lines are a conceptual planning exercise, not official BMRCL plans.

This tool is for general information only and is not financial, investment, legal or real-estate advice.
