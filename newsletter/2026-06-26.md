# This week in Bengaluru property

Bengaluru’s real estate market remains decisively heated in mid-2026, characterized by a significant 33% year-on-year sales jump and a launch pipeline increasingly skewed toward the premium and luxury segments. While East Bengaluru continues to lead price appreciation—with Gunjur recording a 13.4% year-on-year increase—the city faces tightening supply and stretched affordability as buyers navigate a market where inventory overhang has compressed to 12 months.

## 🌡️ Market heat
Bengaluru is decisively heating up in mid-2026: ~24% YoY price growth and ~33% YoY sales jump put it out of step with a softer pan-India market. Fundamentals are still sound — inventory overhang has actually tightened to ~12 months, housing GNPAs remain low at ~1.3%, and the repo rate is comfortable at 5.25% — but launch mix is now ~68% premium/luxury, the asking-vs-registry gap forced a 6–15% guidance-value hike, and affordability is being stretched by the price surge. Risk skews to a luxury-segment air pocket if rates rise or IT hiring softens, since mid-income supply has thinned. _(indicators: 2 red / 2 amber / 2 green; typical home-loan rate ~8; updated 2026-06-14.)_
