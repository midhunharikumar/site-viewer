Is Bangalore property overheating? — Heat index

Updated 2026-06-14 · typical home-loan rate 8.0%

Verdict. Bengaluru is decisively heating up in mid-2026: ~24% YoY price growth and ~33% YoY sales jump put it out of step with a softer pan-India market. Fundamentals are still sound — inventory overhang has actually tightened to ~12 months, housing GNPAs remain low at ~1.3%, and the repo rate is comfortable at 5.25% — but launch mix is now ~68% premium/luxury, the asking-vs-registry gap forced a 6–15% guidance-value hike, and affordability is being stretched by the price surge. Risk skews to a luxury-segment air pocket if rates rise or IT hiring softens, since mid-income supply has thinned.

Asking vs registered price gap
Karnataka hiked guidance values 6–15% in Feb 2026 because the gap to actual transaction prices had widened materially in East and North Bengaluru. Registration fee also doubled (1% → 2%) in Aug 2025 — both signs of an overheated transaction market the state is moving to capture.
